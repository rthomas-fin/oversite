Private workspace

OverSite Financial Command Center is a personal planning and document-management workspace. Access requires an authorized Google account and the workspace owner's permission. The current backend permits only the configured owner. The public website address does not grant access to private records.

Documents and planning results

Upload only information you are permitted to store and process. Supported documents can create review records, update clearly identified AR/AP invoices or produce form drafts. Check the source documents and relevant amounts, dates and answers before relying on a result. OCR and field matching can make mistakes or leave information unresolved.

Cash forecasts depend on entered balances, payment dates and planning assumptions. They do not confirm a bank balance, supplier credit approval or a payment. The app does not execute payments, submit credit applications or sign agreements. A saved draft remains a draft for the user to review.

Google connection and availability

The workspace depends on Google authorization, Google services, GitHub Pages and network availability. Expired or revoked access, provider limits or service interruptions can delay processing. Check the on-screen save and processing status before closing work. Use the export function when an independent backup is needed.

Privacy and contact

The privacy notice describes data access, storage, retention and revocation. Questions about the workspace can be sent to rthomas@oversiteai.io.
